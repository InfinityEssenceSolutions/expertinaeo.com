FAQ

SaaS AEO — frequently asked questions

How is SaaS AEO different from B2B AEO?
SaaS AEO emphasizes feature-page engineering, comparison content, alternative queries and developer documentation. Buyer behaviour is highly product-feature-driven and AI-assistant-researched.
Should we publish comparison content vs competitors?
Yes, with editorial integrity. Honest, well-researched comparison content earns citations and brand trust. Misleading comparisons are detected and penalized.
How do you handle alternative-to-competitor queries?
With care. We engineer content positioned for these queries without misleading framing or trademark issues. The discipline is intent coverage, not deception.
Do you optimize developer documentation for AI?
Yes — increasingly important. AI assistants cite developer documentation for technical queries. We engineer API docs and guides for extractability.
Can you help with our integration marketplace?
Yes. Per-integration pages with proper schema and extractable answer patterns are a high-leverage AEO asset for SaaS.
