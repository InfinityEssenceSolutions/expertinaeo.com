Local search considerations

Personal injury AEO is jurisdictional — content must reflect state law (statute of limitations, comparative negligence, damage caps). Firms serving multiple states need state-specific content modules with proper hreflang or distinct URLs. Local-intent queries ("personal injury lawyer [city]") require LocalBusiness schema and review management.

Structured data opportunities

Recommended schema types: LegalService, Attorney, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, Person (with bar credentials)

Compliance considerations

Personal injury content is YMYL and legally sensitive. All content is reviewed by your attorneys before publication. Case-value content must comply with ABA rules — no specific dollar promises, no guaranteed outcomes. State-specific content requires review for states where your attorneys are admitted. Past results do not guarantee future outcomes — required disclaimers in all case-result content.

FAQ

Personal Injury Law AEO — frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee AI citations for legal content?
No — YMYL legal content is held to strict source quality standards. We engineer your content, schema and E-E-A-T signals to maximize citation probability. AI assistants make the final choice.
Do my attorneys need to write the content?
Attorneys should review and approve all legal content. Authorship can be attributed to attorneys with proper bylines, Person schema, bar admission credentials and sameAs links to authoritative external profiles. We never invent attorney credentials.
How do you handle ABA rules on case results?
All case-value and case-result content complies with ABA rules — no specific dollar promises, no guaranteed outcomes, "past results do not guarantee future outcomes" disclaimers where required. Your compliance team reviews before publication.
How do you handle multi-state personal injury firms?
Multi-state firms benefit from state-specific content modules (statute of limitations, comparative negligence, damage caps) with proper hreflang or distinct URLs. We only produce state content for states where your attorneys are admitted.
How long until I see personal injury AEO results?
Most personal injury firms see measurable AI-answer citation lift within 12–20 weeks. YMYL legal content takes longer than non-YMYL verticals to establish authority. Jurisdictional content typically responds faster than competitive case-value queries.
