Local search considerations

Roofing AEO is intensely local — most queries are local-intent ("roofing contractor [city]", "roof repair near me"). LocalBusiness schema, Google Business Profile optimization and review management are essential. Climate-specific content matters — Florida hurricane roofing differs from Colorado hail roofing. Storm-response content cadence is critical.

Structured data opportunities

Recommended schema types: LocalBusiness, RoofingContractor, Service, FAQPage, OfferCatalog, BreadcrumbList

Compliance considerations

Roofing content requires contractor license disclosure where applicable. Insurance content must be honest — not legal advice. Storm-response content must not pressure vulnerable homeowners. We do not provide roofing advice — we engineer content for your licensed roofers to review and approve.

FAQ

Roofing AEO — frequently asked questions

How is roofing AEO different from general contractor AEO?
Roofing AEO is bifurcated: emergency-intent (storm damage) and planned-intent (replacement, cost). Storm-response content cadence is critical — content must be ready before weather events, not created in their wake.
Should I publish roofing prices?
Honest price ranges for replacement by material type and size are valuable. Exact quotes require inspection. We recommend ranges with "what affects price" explanations — deceptive low-balling is detected and penalized.
How do you handle insurance content?
We produce honest insurance process content with clear disclaimers that this is not legal advice. State-specific insurance content requires review by your compliance team for states you serve.
What about storm-response content?
We engineer storm-response content templates in advance — hail, wind, tree damage. After weather events, content is deployed rapidly with localized updates. Storm content created after the fact is too late — pre-engineering is essential.
How long until I see roofing AEO results?
Most roofing clients see measurable local AI-answer citation lift within 8–14 weeks. Storm-response content can produce immediate visibility after weather events. Cost and material queries take longer to establish authority.
