The challenge

Healthcare content lives under YMYL classification

Healthcare content lives under YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) classification, where answer engines apply stricter source quality standards. Without strong author credentials, source citations, transparent editorial policies and external authority signals, even excellent clinical content is bypassed for a competitor with stronger trust signals.

Our approach

We track healthcare-specific AEO metrics: YMYL query snippet share, AI-answer citation frequency for clinical queries, author authority scores, source-citation compliance and patient-form attribution from answer-engine referrals.

FAQ

Healthcare AEO — frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee healthcare content will be cited?
No — and we distrust any vendor who claims this. We engineer your content, schema and trust signals to maximize citation probability. Answer engines make the final choice, particularly for YMYL.
How do you handle clinical accuracy?
We do not provide clinical advice. We engineer editorial processes, source citation standards and author credentialing. Clinical content is reviewed by your clinical team.
Do you produce clinician-authored content?
We work with your clinical reviewers. Where appropriate, we can engage credentialed external reviewers. We never invent clinician authors or credentials.
How do you handle HIPAA in AEO work?
We do not collect or process patient data in AEO work. We ensure your schema and content practices align with HIPAA considerations for healthcare digital presence. We work with your compliance team.
What about competitor content with weaker credentials?
Competitor content with weaker credentials may be cited today, but YMYL standards tighten over time. Early investment in strong E-E-A-T signals compounds; late movers compete against entrenched authority.
