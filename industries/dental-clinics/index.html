Local search considerations

Dental AEO blends local-intent ("dentist near me", "[city] dental implants") and topical-intent ("how much do implants cost") queries. Local-intent requires LocalBusiness schema, Google Business Profile optimization and review management. Topical-intent requires clinician-authored content with strong E-E-A-T signals — practices without clinician bylines struggle to win.

Structured data opportunities

Recommended schema types: Dentist, Physician, MedicalOrganization, FAQPage, Service, OfferCatalog, BreadcrumbList, Person (with credentials)

Compliance considerations

Dental content is YMYL. We do not provide clinical advice. All clinical content is reviewed by your DDS/DMD before publication. Cost ranges must be honestly represented — deceptive pricing damages both patients and your authority. State dental board advertising rules vary — your compliance team reviews before publication.

FAQ

Dental Clinics AEO — frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee AI citations for my dental content?
No — YMYL content is held to stricter source quality standards. We engineer your content, schema and E-E-A-T signals to maximize citation probability. Answer engines make the final choice.
Do my dentists need to write the content?
Dentists should review and approve clinical content. Authorship can be attributed to your DDS/DMD with proper bylines and Person schema. We engineer content for your clinical team to review — we never invent clinician credentials.
Should I publish dental treatment prices?
Honest price ranges are valuable and citable. Exact quotes require consultation. We recommend ranges with clear "what affects price" explanations — deceptive low-balling is detected and penalized by both patients and engines.
How does HIPAA affect dental AEO work?
We do not collect or process patient data in AEO work. We ensure your schema, content and analytics practices align with HIPAA considerations for healthcare digital presence. We work with your compliance team.
What about state dental board advertising rules?
State dental boards have specific advertising rules (testimonials, claims, before/after). We work with your compliance team to ensure all content complies with rules in states where you operate. We do not provide legal advice.
