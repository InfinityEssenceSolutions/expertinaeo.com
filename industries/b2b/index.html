The challenge

B2B AEO is dominated by decision-stage queries

B2B AEO is dominated by decision-stage queries: comparison ("X vs Y"), alternative ("alternative to X"), capability ("does X do Y") and integration ("does X integrate with Y"). Brands that engineer content for these queries earn AI-answer citations that compound into RFP shortlist inclusion; brands that don't are absent from the consideration set.

Our approach

We track B2B-specific AEO metrics: comparison-page snippet share, alternative-query AI-answer frequency, capability-query visibility, integration-query citation share, thought-leadership citation frequency and demo-form attribution from answer-engine referrals.

FAQ

B2B AEO — frequently asked questions

How is B2B AEO different from SaaS AEO?
Heavy overlap — SaaS is a B2B sub-segment. Non-SaaS B2B (professional services, manufacturing, distribution) has different buyer behaviour, longer sales cycles and different content patterns. We tailor methodology to your B2B segment.
Should we publish comparison content vs competitors?
Yes, with editorial integrity. Honest comparison content earns citations. Deceptive comparison framing is detected and penalized — and creates legal risk in some jurisdictions.
How do you handle B2B thought leadership AEO?
Author-attributed content with strong E-E-A-T signals. Executive bylines, author schema with sameAs, source citations on claims. Compounds into citation authority for the brand.
What about gated content and AEO?
Gated content is invisible to AI assistants. We architect ungated summary content with extractable answer patterns that lead to gated assets. The ungated content earns citations; the gated asset captures leads.
How do you measure B2B AEO impact?
Beyond AEO metrics, we tie answer-engine referral traffic to demo requests, RFP submissions and pipeline. Attribution discipline distinguishes AEO impact from other channels.
