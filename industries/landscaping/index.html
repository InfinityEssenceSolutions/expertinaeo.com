Local search considerations

Landscaping AEO is intensely local and climate-specific. Plant selection, watering needs and maintenance cadence vary by USDA hardiness zone. Service-area pages must use LocalBusiness schema with consistent NAP. Climate-specific content differentiates from national competitors.

Structured data opportunities

Recommended schema types: LocalBusiness, LandscapingService, Service, FAQPage, OfferCatalog, BreadcrumbList

Compliance considerations

Landscaping content requires contractor license disclosure where applicable (irrigation work, pesticide application). Plant recommendations must be climate-appropriate — recommending zone 8 plants for zone 5 customers damages trust. We do not provide horticultural advice — we engineer content for your licensed landscapers to review and approve.

FAQ

Landscaping AEO — frequently asked questions

How is landscaping AEO different from general local services AEO?
Landscaping AEO is bifurcated: routine-maintenance-intent and project-intent queries. Climate-specific content matters significantly — Charlotte landscaping (zone 7b/8a) differs from Phoenix landscaping (zone 9b). USDA hardiness zone-specific plant content differentiates from national competitors.
Should I publish landscaping prices?
Honest price ranges for common maintenance (mowing, fertilization) and project types (lawn install, hardscape) are valuable. Exact quotes require site visit. We recommend ranges with "what affects price" explanations.
How do you handle climate-specific content?
Per-climate-zone content with USDA hardiness zone context. Plant recommendations are zone-appropriate — we never recommend zone 8 plants for zone 5 customers. Climate-specific maintenance cadence for each service area.
How do you handle seasonal pressure?
We plan seasonal content calendars (spring cleanup, summer maintenance, fall leaf removal, winter prep) and ensure content is engineered before peak pressure. Climate-specific cadence for each service area.
How long until I see landscaping AEO results?
Most landscaping clients see measurable local AI-answer citation lift within 8–14 weeks. Maintenance queries typically respond faster; project-intent and cost queries take longer to establish authority.
