Real estate AEO is dominated by local and neighborhood-level queries

Real estate AEO is dominated by local and neighborhood-level queries. Buyers ask "is X neighborhood good for families", "school ratings in Y", "average rent in Z". Brands that engineer content for these queries win visibility that compounds into lead flow; brands that don't lose to portals with stronger topical coverage.

FAQ

Real Estate AEO — frequently asked questions

How do you handle neighborhood content at scale?
With editorial discipline. We do not produce generic neighborhood pages. We engineer genuine content per neighborhood with local expertise — either your agents' insights or our editorial research. Quality over quantity.
Do you optimize for Zillow, Redfin, etc?
We optimize your direct site for AI-answer citations where AI assistants cite branded sources. Portal optimization (Zillow, Redfin) is a separate discipline; we can advise.
How do you handle listing schema?
Comprehensive schema on listing pages including property attributes, geo, offers and availability. Validated against schema.org specifications and Google Merchant Center where applicable.
What about voice search for real estate?
Voice is growing in real estate. "Homes for sale in X", "how much is rent in Y" — we engineer single-answer content for these queries.
Can you handle multi-market real estate brands?
Yes. Multi-market real estate brands benefit from our international AEO methodology adapted to local markets. Hreflang, local entity signals and localized content per market.
