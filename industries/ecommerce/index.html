FAQ

eCommerce AEO — frequently asked questions

How is eCommerce AEO different from SaaS AEO?
eCommerce AEO emphasizes product-page schema, best-X-for-Y queries, voice shopping, local inventory and review markup. Intent is transactional; content is product-led.
Do you optimize for voice shopping?
Yes. Voice shopping is growing. We engineer single-answer content for product recommendation and "add to cart" voice queries.
What about Amazon vs our direct site?
Both. We optimize your direct site for AI-answer citations where AI assistants cite branded sources. Amazon content requires a separate playbook we can advise on.
How do you handle product schema?
Comprehensive Product schema with offers, reviews, ratings, availability and identifiers. Validated against schema.org and Google Merchant Center requirements.
Should we publish comparison content vs competitors?
Yes, with editorial integrity. Honest comparison content earns citations. Deceptive comparison framing is detected and penalized.
