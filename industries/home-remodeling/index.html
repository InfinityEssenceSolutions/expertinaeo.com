Local search considerations

Home remodeling AEO blends local-intent ("remodeling contractor [city]", "kitchen remodel [city]") and topical-intent ("how much does a kitchen remodel cost") queries. Local-intent requires LocalBusiness schema, Google Business Profile and portfolio review management. Regional cost variation matters — San Francisco kitchen remodels cost differently than Charlotte kitchen remodels.

Structured data opportunities

Recommended schema types: LocalBusiness, HomeAndConstructionBusiness, Service, FAQPage, OfferCatalog, BreadcrumbList, ImageObject (for portfolio)

Compliance considerations

Home remodeling content requires contractor license disclosure where applicable. Cost content must be honest — not low-balling to win leads. ROI content must use reputable sources (Remodeling Magazine Cost vs Value Report) — not inflated. Portfolio content must be your own work — not stock photography presented as your projects.

FAQ

Home Remodeling AEO — frequently asked questions

How is home remodeling AEO different from general contractor AEO?
Home remodeling AEO is dominated by high-consideration queries (cost, timeline, contractor selection). Cost content is particularly high-leverage — AI assistants increasingly cite honest, specific cost ranges. Regional cost variation matters — content must reflect local market realities.
Should I publish remodeling prices?
Honest price ranges for common project types (kitchen, bathroom, addition) are valuable. Exact quotes require design and material specification. We recommend ranges with "what affects price" explanations — deceptive low-balling is detected and penalized.
How do you handle portfolio content?
Portfolio content uses your own project photos with proper alt text, before/after context and CreativeWork schema. We do not use stock photography presented as your work — that's deceptive and damages authority.
How do you handle ROI content?
ROI content uses reputable sources (Remodeling Magazine Cost vs Value Report) with regional variation. We do not inflate ROI claims — AI assistants and discerning homeowners detect exaggerated claims.
How long until I see home remodeling AEO results?
Most remodeling contractors see measurable AI-answer citation lift within 10–16 weeks. Cost and timeline queries typically respond faster; contractor-selection queries take longer to establish authority.
