Local search considerations

Med-spa AEO blends local-intent ("med-spa near me", "Botox [city]") and topical-intent ("how much does Botox cost") queries. Local-intent requires LocalBusiness schema, Google Business Profile and review management. Topical-intent requires clinician-authored content with strong E-E-A-T signals — med-spas without medical director bylines struggle to win.

Structured data opportunities

Recommended schema types: MedicalOrganization, Physician, Service, FAQPage, OfferCatalog, BreadcrumbList, Person (with credentials)

Compliance considerations

Med-spa content is YMYL and requires medical director oversight. All clinical content is reviewed by your medical director before publication. Treatment-comparison content must be honest — not manufacturer-marketed positioning. Before/after content requires proper disclaimers and compliance with state medical board rules. We do not provide medical advice — we engineer content for your medical team to review and approve.

FAQ

Medical Spas AEO — frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee AI citations for med-spa content?
No — YMYL med-spa content is held to strict source quality standards. We engineer your content, schema and E-E-A-T signals to maximize citation probability. AI assistants make the final choice.
Does my medical director need to write the content?
Your medical director should review and approve all clinical content. Authorship can be attributed to practitioners with proper bylines, Person schema and credentials. We never invent credentials.
Should I publish med-spa prices?
Honest price ranges are valuable and citable. Med-spa pricing is typically unit-based (Botox per unit) or treatment-area-based — we recommend ranges with clear pricing-structure explanations.
How do you handle before/after content?
Before/after content requires proper disclaimers, individual result variability statements and compliance with state medical board rules. Your compliance team reviews before publication.
How long until I see med-spa AEO results?
Most med-spa clients see measurable AI-answer citation lift within 12–18 weeks. YMYL med-spa content takes longer than non-YMYL verticals to establish authority. Treatment-comparison queries typically respond faster than eligibility or cost queries.
