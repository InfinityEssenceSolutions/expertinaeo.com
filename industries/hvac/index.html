Local search considerations

HVAC AEO is intensely local and seasonal. Service-area pages must use LocalBusiness schema with consistent NAP. Climate-specific content matters — Las Vegas AC content differs from Tampa dehumidification content. Seasonal content cadence (spring AC prep, fall furnace prep) compounds topical authority. Multi-state operators need climate-specific content per service area.

Structured data opportunities

Recommended schema types: LocalBusiness, HVACBusiness, Service, FAQPage, OfferCatalog, BreadcrumbList

Compliance considerations

HVAC content requires contractor license disclosure where applicable. Load calculation content should reference ACCA Manual J — not generic tonnage rules. We do not provide HVAC installation advice — we engineer content for your licensed technicians to review and approve.

FAQ

HVAC AEO — frequently asked questions

How is HVAC AEO different from general local services AEO?
HVAC AEO is intensely seasonal — demand spikes 10x in summer and winter. Repair-vs-replace and cost queries dominate. Climate-specific content (desert AC, humid-climate dehumidification) differentiates from generic competitors.
Should I publish HVAC prices?
Honest price ranges for diagnostic, common repairs and replacement are valuable. Exact quotes require inspection. We recommend ranges with "what affects price" explanations.
How do you handle seasonal HVAC demand?
We plan seasonal content calendars (spring AC prep, fall furnace prep) and ensure emergency-service content is engineered for extraction before peak demand. Pre-season content engineering is critical — content created during peak is too late.
Do you handle multi-state HVAC businesses?
Yes. Multi-state HVAC operators benefit from climate-specific content per service area, per-location LocalBusiness schema and seasonal cadence tuned to each climate.
How long until I see HVAC AEO results?
Most HVAC clients see measurable local AI-answer citation lift within 8–14 weeks. Emergency-service queries typically respond faster; cost and replacement-decision queries take longer.
