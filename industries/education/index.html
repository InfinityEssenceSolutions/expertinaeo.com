FAQ

Education AEO — frequently asked questions

How is education AEO different from SaaS AEO?
Education AEO spans a longer decision journey with multiple stakeholders (student, parent, advisor). Content is program-led with strong cost/transparency expectations. E-E-A-T matters particularly for accredited institutions.
Do you handle international student AEO?
Yes. International student AEO requires language-specific content, visa and credential coverage and proper hreflang for source markets. We work with your international enrollment team.
What about ranking content vs competitors?
Ranking content requires editorial integrity. "Best X program" content must be honestly researched and clearly sourced. Deceptive ranking content is detected and penalized.
How do you handle financial aid content?
With care. Financial aid content must be accurate, current and clearly sourced. Schema and content practices align with regulatory requirements for educational institutions.
Can you help with continuing education AEO?
Yes. Continuing education, professional development and certification programs have distinct AEO patterns. We scope engagements to your specific education segment.
