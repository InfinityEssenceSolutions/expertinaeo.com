FAQ

Pricing — frequently asked questions

How much does AEO cost?
Our packages start at $1,000 per month (introductory price) for AEO Starter, $2,000 per month for AEO Growth (most popular) and $4,000 per month for AEO Authority. All packages include a 20% introductory discount on the first three months.
Why is there a three-month minimum?
Answer-engine signals take time to take effect. A three-month minimum allows sufficient time for content engineering, schema implementation and entity-signal strengthening to produce measurable progress. We commit to baseline measurement and periodic re-measurement — never to guaranteed outcomes.
Can I pay monthly?
Yes. All packages are billed monthly. The monthly invoice is due at the beginning of each service month. You receive a monthly written report before the next billing cycle.
Do I need to pay three months upfront?
No. You pay monthly. You do NOT need to pay the entire three-month contract upfront. For AEO Growth as an example: month 1 is $2,000, month 2 is $2,000, month 3 is $2,000 — total minimum commitment $6,000, paid across three months.
What happens after three months?
Service may continue at the standard monthly rate ($1,250 for Starter, $2,500 for Growth, $5,000 for Authority), subject to agreed renewal and cancellation terms. We review progress against baseline at month three and discuss continuation, scope adjustment or scaling.
Is AEO different from SEO?
Yes — they are related but distinct. SEO optimizes for ranked page results; AEO optimizes for extracted and cited answers (featured snippets, People Also Ask, voice, AI Overviews). The two share foundations (technical crawlability, content quality, structured data) but AEO adds deeper schema work, answer-first content patterns and explicit entity-authority work.
Is content writing included?
All packages include answer-focused content briefs (writing specifications), not fully written articles. This is intentional: briefs allow your team or ours to execute with editorial discipline. Additional content writing beyond package scope is quoted separately.
Are AI rankings guaranteed?
No. Answer engines make the final citation choice. We commit to methodology, baseline measurement and periodic re-measurement — never to guaranteed AI visibility, rankings or revenue outcomes. We distrust any vendor who promises guaranteed AEO results.
Can I upgrade my package?
Yes. You can upgrade mid-engagement — we apply a pro-rated credit for unused Starter or Growth fees toward the upgraded package. Downgrades are handled at the next renewal boundary.
Are technical implementations included?
Schema implementation is included within the package limits (3, 8 or 16 pages). Other technical implementations (site speed, server-side rendering, CMS changes) are recommendations only — your dev team executes them. We can quote separately for hands-on technical implementation.
Do you work with international businesses?
Yes. We serve businesses in the USA, UK, UAE, Canada, Australia, India, Singapore and Germany. We do not invent local offices — strong entity signals and localized content are sufficient. See our international AEO services for market-specific methodology.
How do you measure progress?
Every engagement begins with an instrumented baseline (AEO readiness score, snippet share, AI-answer citation frequency where measurable, organic to answer-engine referral traffic). We re-measure at agreed intervals and report against the baseline with methodology disclosed. We distinguish changes caused by our work from seasonal, algorithmic and competitive effects.
