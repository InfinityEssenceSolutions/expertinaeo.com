FAQ

Pricing questions, answered plainly

How much does AEO cost?
Packages start at $1,000/mo (Starter, intro), $2,000/mo (Growth, most popular) and $4,000/mo (Authority). All include a 20% introductory discount on the first three months.
Why a 3-month minimum?
Answer-engine signals take time. Three months gives content engineering, schema and entity work enough runway to produce measurable progress — we commit to measurement, never to outcomes.
Do I pay upfront?
No. You pay monthly. You never pay the full three-month contract upfront.
What happens after month 3?
Continue at standard pricing ($1,250 / $2,500 / $5,000), adjust scope, or stop. No automatic renewal — we review progress and agree the next phase together.
Is content writing included?
Packages include answer-focused content briefs (writing specs), not finished articles. Additional writing is quoted separately.
Are AI rankings guaranteed?
No. Answer engines choose the citation. We commit to methodology and honest measurement — we distrust anyone promising guaranteed AEO results.
Can I upgrade mid-engagement?
Yes. We apply a pro-rated credit toward the upgraded package. Downgrades happen at the next renewal boundary.
Are technical implementations included?
Schema is included within package limits. Site speed, SSR and CMS changes are recommendations — your dev team executes. Hands-on implementation is quoted separately.
Do you work internationally?
Yes — USA, UK, UAE, Canada, Australia, India, Singapore and Germany. No invented local offices.
How do you measure progress?
Instrumented baseline (AEO score, snippet share, answer citations, referral traffic), re-measured at agreed intervals with methodology disclosed and attribution discipline applied.
