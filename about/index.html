About ExpertinAEO

A focused AEO practice, backed by 23+ years of Tapadia Tech

ExpertinAEO is the dedicated Answer Engine Optimization practice of Tapadia Tech. We bring the rigour of an established technology consulting firm to a modern discipline.

Our story

Born from an established digital consultancy

Tapadia Tech has been serving international clients across digital marketing, SEO, performance marketing and software development since 2001. Our sister brand, ExpertinSEO, has been serving SEO clients since 2008.

As search shifted from ranked page results to extracted answers — featured snippets, People Also Ask, voice search, AI-generated answers — we recognized that the disciplines required to win on these surfaces were not the same as conventional SEO. They required deeper structured-data expertise, more sophisticated content engineering and a stronger focus on entity authority.

ExpertinAEO was created as a focused practice to bring specialist depth to this discipline. We are not a generalist SEO shop with an AEO bolt-on. Every strategist, writer and developer on our team is hired and trained for AEO.

What we don't do

Our non-negotiables

We don't fabricate results

Every case study, testimonial and metric we publish is verified. Where we do not have evidence, we publish our methodology instead of inventing success stories.

We don't claim fake AI output

Our free AEO Checker performs a real server-side fetch and analysis. Our FAQ Generator uses deterministic question-pattern generation — not fake "AI generation" without an AI model.

We don't invent local presence

We do not invent local offices, customers or testimonials. Strong entity signals and localized content are sufficient. We are clear about where we do and do not have local presence.

We don't guarantee outcomes

Answer engines make the final citation choice. We measure, diagnose and improve. We commit to methodology and reporting — never to guaranteed AI visibility or ranking.
