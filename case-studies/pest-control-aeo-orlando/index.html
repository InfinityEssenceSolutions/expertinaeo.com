Business context

The business situation

A pest control business serving the Orlando metro area since 2013, operating with 14 technicians and 3 field supervisors. The business offers general pest control, termite treatment, mosquito control, and rodent control for residential and commercial properties.

The challenge

Initial challenge

Orlando's subtropical climate creates year-round pest pressure — termites, mosquitoes, roaches, ants, rodents. The business competes against national pest-control chains (Orkin, Terminix) and local competitors. Seasonal pressure (mosquitoes in summer, rodents in fall, termites in spring) creates time-sensitive demand. National competitors have larger content budgets; local operators win with climate-specific content.

The opportunity

AEO opportunity

Florida-specific pest content (subtropical termites, year-round mosquitoes) is differentiated from generic national content. Identification content ("what are these tiny bugs in my kitchen") is high voice-search volume. Cost-transparency content for common treatments is highly citable. Seasonal prevention content compounds authority over time.

Baseline audit

Website and content audit findings

A baseline AEO Checker scan would show: AEO Readiness Score approximately 37/100. Critical gaps: no pest-identification content, no Florida-specific pest content, no LocalBusiness schema on service-area pages, no cost-transparency content, no FAQPage schema. Strengths: 11+ years of operation, strong Google Business Profile with 160+ reviews averaging 4.7 stars.

Note on baseline scores. The AEO Readiness Score referenced above is an estimated baseline based on typical industry patterns. An actual engagement begins with a real instrumented baseline measurement using our AEO Checker methodology.
Strategy

Proposed strategy

Three-phase strategy: (1) Identification content — per-pest identification library with photos (licensed), signs and behavior. (2) Cost and comparison content — treatment-cost ranges with "what affects price" explanations, treatment-comparison content (chemical vs bait vs natural). (3) Seasonal and regional content — Florida-specific pest-pressure content, seasonal prevention content calendar.

Implementation

Implementation work

Month 1: Baseline measurement, LocalBusiness schema across 6 service-area pages, FAQ content for top 15 Orlando pest control queries. Month 2: Pest-identification content library (12 pests with licensed photos), cost-transparency content (6 articles). Month 3: Treatment-comparison content (5 articles), seasonal prevention content (8 articles), monthly performance report.

Timeline

Implementation timeline

3 months minimum engagement. Phase 1 (identification) weeks 1-4. Phase 2 (cost and comparison) weeks 5-8. Phase 3 (seasonal content) weeks 9-12 with ongoing quarterly refresh and pre-season content cadence.

Measurement

Measurement methodology

Baseline measurement: AEO Readiness Score, pest-identification snippet share, treatment-cost AI-answer citation frequency, seasonal-query visibility, local PAA capture. Re-measurement at months 1, 2, 3. Methodology disclosed; attribution discipline applied.

Honest measurement commitment. We do not present fabricated before-and-after metrics, AI citations, rankings or revenue figures. Real engagements report against an instrumented baseline with methodology disclosed and attribution discipline applied.
Proposed takeaways

Key takeaways (proposed, not measured)

These takeaways represent the proposed strategic direction for this type of engagement. They are NOT measured outcomes from a completed client engagement.

  • Florida-specific pest content (subtropical termites, year-round mosquitoes) differentiates from generic national content and is highly citable.
  • Pest-identification content ("what are these tiny bugs in my kitchen") is high voice-search volume and represents untapped opportunity for local operators.
  • Cost-transparency and treatment-comparison content are among the highest AI-citation opportunities in pest control AEO.
  • Seasonal content cadence (pre-spring termites, pre-summer mosquitoes) is essential — content created during peak is too late.
