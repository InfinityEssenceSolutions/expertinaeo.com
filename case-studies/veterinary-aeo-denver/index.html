Business context

The business situation

A veterinary clinic in Denver operating since 2014 with 3 DVMs, 4 veterinary technicians, and 2 receptionists. The clinic offers general wellness, surgery, dental care, and emergency services during business hours. Strong client base but limited content for AI-assisted discovery.

The challenge

Initial challenge

Denver's pet-owning population is among the highest in the US — over 60% of households have pets. The clinic competes against corporate veterinary networks (Banfield, VCA) and other independent clinics. YMYL-adjacent content requires DVM oversight; clinics without veterinarian bylines cannot establish authority. Emergency-care queries are time-sensitive and require clear "when to seek immediate care" boundaries.

The opportunity

AEO opportunity

Denver-specific pet content (high-altitude considerations, regional parasites) is differentiated. Wellness-cadence content with species and age specifications is winnable. Cost-transparency content for routine services is highly citable. Emergency-care content with proper DVM authorship is essential.

Baseline audit

Website and content audit findings

A baseline AEO Checker scan would show: AEO Readiness Score approximately 34/100. Critical gaps: no DVM bylines, no Person schema for veterinarians, no Denver-specific content, no emergency-care content, no FAQPage schema, no editorial policy. Strengths: 3 DVMs with strong credentials, 10+ years of operation, Google Business Profile with 120+ reviews averaging 4.8 stars.

Note on baseline scores. The AEO Readiness Score referenced above is an estimated baseline based on typical industry patterns. An actual engagement begins with a real instrumented baseline measurement using our AEO Checker methodology.
Strategy

Proposed strategy

Three-phase strategy: (1) E-E-A-T foundation — DVM bylines with Person schema, practitioner credentials pages, editorial policy with DVM oversight. (2) Service content library — per-service pages with DVM bylines, wellness-cadence content with species and age specifications. (3) Emergency and cost content — emergency-care content with clear "when to seek immediate care" boundaries, cost-transparency content for routine services.

Implementation

Implementation work

Month 1: Baseline measurement, DVM bylines with Person schema for 3 veterinarians, editorial policy and DVM oversight protocol. Month 2: 8 service pages with DVM bylines, FAQ content for top 15 Denver veterinary queries. Month 3: Emergency-care content (6 articles), cost-transparency content (5 articles), Denver-specific pet content (4 articles), monthly performance report.

Timeline

Implementation timeline

3 months minimum engagement. Phase 1 (E-E-A-T foundation) weeks 1-4. Phase 2 (service content) weeks 5-8. Phase 3 (emergency and cost) weeks 9-12 with ongoing quarterly refresh. All clinical content reviewed by DVM team before publication.

Measurement

Measurement methodology

Baseline measurement: AEO Readiness Score, service-page snippet share, emergency-care AI-answer citation frequency, cost-query visibility, DVM byline authority scores. Re-measurement at months 1, 2, 3. Methodology disclosed; attribution discipline applied. All clinical content reviewed by DVM team before publication.

Honest measurement commitment. We do not present fabricated before-and-after metrics, AI citations, rankings or revenue figures. Real engagements report against an instrumented baseline with methodology disclosed and attribution discipline applied.
Proposed takeaways

Key takeaways (proposed, not measured)

These takeaways represent the proposed strategic direction for this type of engagement. They are NOT measured outcomes from a completed client engagement.

  • Veterinary clinics cannot win YMYL-adjacent AI-answer citations without DVM bylines and proper E-E-A-T signals.
  • Emergency-care content with clear "when to seek immediate care" boundaries is essential — delaying treatment with online research is harmful.
  • Denver-specific pet content (high-altitude considerations, regional parasites) differentiates from generic national content.
  • Cost-transparency content for routine services is increasingly cited by AI assistants and builds trust with pet owners.
