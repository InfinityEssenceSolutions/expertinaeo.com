Business context

The business situation

A home remodeling business in Austin specializing in kitchen and bathroom remodels, operating since 2015 with 9 employees including 2 designers and 6 tradespeople. The business has a strong project portfolio but limited content infrastructure for cost and timeline queries.

The challenge

Initial challenge

Austin's housing market boom has created intense demand for remodeling services — over 1,200 licensed remodelers operate in the metro area. The business competes against national franchises and design-build firms with significant content budgets. Cost and timeline queries ("how much does a kitchen remodel cost in Austin") are highly competitive against national content libraries with regional variation.

The opportunity

AEO opportunity

Austin-specific cost content (regional labor and material costs) is differentiated from generic national content. Timeline content with project-phase breakdown is high AI-citation potential. Material-comparison content (quartz vs granite) is winnable. Contractor-selection content with selection criteria builds trust and authority.

Baseline audit

Website and content audit findings

A baseline AEO Checker scan would show: AEO Readiness Score approximately 36/100. Critical gaps: no cost-transparency content, no Austin-specific regional content, no timeline content, no material-comparison content, no LocalBusiness schema on service-area pages, no FAQPage schema. Strengths: 9+ years of operation, strong project portfolio, Google Business Profile with 140+ reviews averaging 4.9 stars.

Note on baseline scores. The AEO Readiness Score referenced above is an estimated baseline based on typical industry patterns. An actual engagement begins with a real instrumented baseline measurement using our AEO Checker methodology.
Strategy

Proposed strategy

Three-phase strategy: (1) Cost and timeline content — Austin-specific cost ranges for kitchen and bathroom remodels, project-phase timeline content. (2) Material and comparison content — material-comparison content (quartz vs granite, etc.), contractor-selection content with criteria. (3) Portfolio and design content — project portfolio with proper CreativeWork schema, design-principle content with "when to hire a professional" boundaries.

Implementation

Implementation work

Month 1: Baseline measurement, LocalBusiness schema across 5 service-area pages, FAQ content for top 15 Austin remodeling queries. Month 2: Austin-specific cost content (6 articles), timeline content (4 articles with project-phase breakdown). Month 3: Material-comparison content (5 articles), contractor-selection content (4 articles), portfolio schema implementation, monthly performance report.

Timeline

Implementation timeline

3 months minimum engagement. Phase 1 (cost and timeline) weeks 1-4. Phase 2 (material and comparison) weeks 5-8. Phase 3 (portfolio and design) weeks 9-12 with ongoing quarterly refresh and portfolio expansion.

Measurement

Measurement methodology

Baseline measurement: AEO Readiness Score, cost-query snippet share, timeline-query AI-answer citation frequency, contractor-selection query visibility, portfolio image visibility. Re-measurement at months 1, 2, 3. Methodology disclosed; attribution discipline applied.

Honest measurement commitment. We do not present fabricated before-and-after metrics, AI citations, rankings or revenue figures. Real engagements report against an instrumented baseline with methodology disclosed and attribution discipline applied.
Proposed takeaways

Key takeaways (proposed, not measured)

These takeaways represent the proposed strategic direction for this type of engagement. They are NOT measured outcomes from a completed client engagement.

  • Austin-specific cost content (regional labor and material costs) differentiates from generic national content and is highly citable.
  • Project-phase timeline content is among the highest AI-citation opportunities in home remodeling AEO.
  • Material-comparison content (quartz vs granite) is winnable with honest engineering integrity, not manufacturer-marketed positioning.
  • Portfolio content with proper CreativeWork schema builds authority — stock photography presented as your work damages trust.
