Business context

The business situation

A medical spa in Scottsdale operating since 2017, with a medical director (MD), 2 nurse practitioners, and 3 aestheticians. The med-spa offers Botox, fillers, laser hair removal, IPL, microneedling, and medical-grade facials. Strong patient base but limited content for treatment-comparison queries.

The challenge

Initial challenge

Scottsdale is a highly competitive med-spa market — over 80 med-spas operate in the area. The business competes against national med-spa chains and aesthetic specialty practices. YMYL-adjacent content requires medical director oversight; med-spas without proper credentials cannot establish authority. Treatment-comparison queries (Botox vs Dysport, laser vs IPL) are highly competitive.

The opportunity

AEO opportunity

Treatment-comparison content with honest product positioning is high AI-citation potential. Eligibility content with medical-contraindication boundaries is winnable. Cost-transparency content with unit pricing (Botox per unit) is increasingly cited. Practitioner credentials content (MD medical director, NPs) establishes YMYL authority.

Baseline audit

Website and content audit findings

A baseline AEO Checker scan would show: AEO Readiness Score approximately 32/100. Critical gaps: no medical director bylines, no Person schema for practitioners, no treatment-comparison content, no eligibility content, no FAQPage schema. Strengths: MD medical director on staff, 7+ years of operation, Google Business Profile with 130+ reviews averaging 4.9 stars.

Note on baseline scores. The AEO Readiness Score referenced above is an estimated baseline based on typical industry patterns. An actual engagement begins with a real instrumented baseline measurement using our AEO Checker methodology.
Strategy

Proposed strategy

Three-phase strategy: (1) E-E-A-T foundation — medical director bylines with Person schema, practitioner credentials pages, editorial policy with medical director oversight. (2) Treatment content library — per-treatment pages with practitioner bylines, treatment-comparison content. (3) Eligibility and cost content — treatment-eligibility content with medical-contraindication boundaries, cost-transparency content with unit pricing.

Implementation

Implementation work

Month 1: Baseline measurement, medical director bylines with Person schema, editorial policy and medical director oversight protocol. Month 2: 8 treatment pages with practitioner bylines, FAQ content for top 15 Scottsdale med-spa queries. Month 3: Treatment-comparison content (5 articles), eligibility content (6 articles), cost-transparency content (4 articles), monthly performance report.

Timeline

Implementation timeline

3 months minimum engagement. Phase 1 (E-E-A-T foundation) weeks 1-4. Phase 2 (treatment content) weeks 5-8. Phase 3 (eligibility and cost) weeks 9-12 with ongoing quarterly refresh. All clinical content reviewed by medical director before publication.

Measurement

Measurement methodology

Baseline measurement: AEO Readiness Score, treatment-comparison snippet share, eligibility-query AI-answer citation frequency, cost-query visibility, practitioner-name authority scores. Re-measurement at months 1, 2, 3. Methodology disclosed; attribution discipline applied. All clinical content reviewed by medical director before publication.

Honest measurement commitment. We do not present fabricated before-and-after metrics, AI citations, rankings or revenue figures. Real engagements report against an instrumented baseline with methodology disclosed and attribution discipline applied.
Proposed takeaways

Key takeaways (proposed, not measured)

These takeaways represent the proposed strategic direction for this type of engagement. They are NOT measured outcomes from a completed client engagement.

  • Med-spas cannot win YMYL-adjacent AI-answer citations without medical director bylines and proper E-E-A-T signals.
  • Treatment-comparison content (Botox vs Dysport, laser vs IPL) is among the highest AI-citation opportunities in med-spa AEO.
  • Treatment-eligibility content with medical-contraindication boundaries is winnable and differentiates from competitors with up-sell positioning.
  • Cost-transparency content with unit pricing is increasingly cited by AI assistants and builds trust with potential clients.
