Our honesty policy on case studies. We do not publish fabricated case studies, invented client logos or unverified metrics. Where we have authorized client engagements with supporting evidence, those case studies are clearly labeled "Confidential Client Case Study". Where we do not, we publish illustrative strategy scenarios — realistic business situations and proposed implementation plans, clearly labeled as such. We never present illustrative scenarios as completed client work.
Methodology

How we evaluate every engagement

Baseline measurement

Every engagement begins with an instrumented baseline — AEO score, snippet share, citation frequency, organic to answer-engine referral traffic.

Periodic re-measurement

Same instrumentation, same definitions, run at agreed intervals. No vanity metrics, no methodology drift between reports.

Attribution discipline

We distinguish changes caused by our work from seasonal, algorithmic and competitive effects. Where attribution is uncertain, we say so.
