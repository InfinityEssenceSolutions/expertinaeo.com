Why is the US market harder for AEO?
The US has the highest density of optimized content per topic, the most sophisticated AI-search adoption and the most demanding buyer behavior. Winning requires not just content but entity authority, schema discipline and structured-data engineering.
Do I need a US office to win US AEO visibility?
No. We do not invent local offices. Strong entity signals (organization schema, consistent NAP, external authority profiles) and well-structured content are sufficient. Local presence helps for local-intent queries but is not required for topical AEO.
How do you handle US-specific regulatory content?
We work with your legal/compliance team to ensure FTC disclosures, HIPAA considerations and state privacy requirements are reflected in your structured data and content. We do not provide legal advice.
What's typical timeline for US AEO lift?
Most US clients see measurable snippet share and AI-answer citation lift within 8–16 weeks. Highly competitive verticals take longer; less competitive ones faster.
