Market overview

What makes India distinct for AEO

India is a mobile-first, voice-heavy market with strong English-language adoption alongside major Indic languages. Multilingual AEO is essential for national reach.

Buyer behaviour: Indian buyers are mobile-first, voice-heavy and price-sensitive. Voice queries in Hindi and major regional languages drive significant discovery. Hindi-language AEO is under-invested.

Primary answer surfaces: Google AI Overviews (English + Hindi), Google featured snippets, PAA, ChatGPT, voice via Google Assistant (English + Hindi).

Competitiveness: High in English; low-moderate in Indic languages — significant early-mover opportunity for Hindi and regional language AEO.

Language and content standards

English (India) + Hindi + major regional languages (Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi). Proper hreflang for each. Devanagari script for Hindi.

Compliance considerations

Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP); RBI regulations for financial content; sector-specific regulators (IRDAI for insurance, TRAI for telecom).

FAQ

India AEO — frequently asked questions

Should I produce Hindi content for Indian AEO?
For national reach, yes. Hindi represents ~40% of India's population and is under-served in AEO. Even brands targeting English-speaking urban buyers benefit from Hindi for adjacent discovery.
How many Indic languages should I target?
Depends on your reach. Tier 1: English + Hindi. Tier 2: add Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi. Tier 3: add Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi. We help you scope based on audience and budget.
Is Indian English different from UK/US English?
Yes — Indian English has its own conventions, vocabulary and idiom. Using UK or US English for Indian audiences is detected and weakens local entity signals.
How important is voice search in India?
Critical. India has high voice-search adoption, particularly in Hindi and regional languages. We engineer single-answer content and entity signals specifically for Indian voice.
