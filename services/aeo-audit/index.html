The discipline

An AEO audit is measurement before recommendations

An AEO audit is a structured assessment of how prepared your website is to be chosen as the cited source by answer engines — AI assistants, voice search, generative search and featured snippets. It examines five categories: technical crawlability, answer content, structured data, entity signals and credibility.

Our audit is not a checklist exercise. It begins with instrumented measurement, identifies the structural patterns that explain why some pages earn citations and others do not, and ends with a prioritized roadmap tied to commercial impact. Every recommendation is grounded in evidence.

When to commission an audit

Most audit engagements begin when a business has invested in AEO tactics but cannot explain their impact, or is about to scale an AEO program and needs a defensible baseline.

What we fix

  • No visibility baseline — You cannot say with confidence what your current answer visibility is, or how it has changed.
  • Tactic fatigue — Schema, FAQ content and entity work have been added with no measurable effect.
  • Mystery pattern — Some pages earn citations, others do not, and no one can explain the pattern.
  • Pre-investment decision — You are about to invest in an AEO program and need a defensible basis for scope and budget.
  • Competitor pressure — Competitors are increasingly cited in AI answers where you are absent.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does an AEO audit take?
Typically 2–4 weeks depending on page inventory size and competitor set. Larger inventories or multi-region audits take longer.
Can I run the free AEO Checker instead?
The free checker scans one page. The paid audit covers your full commercial page set, competitors and topic clusters — with prioritized recommendations and a re-measurement protocol.
Does the audit include implementation?
No. The audit is a diagnostic and recommendation engagement. Implementation is a separate engagement, though many clients retain us to execute the roadmap.
How much does an audit cost?
Fixed fee, scoped to your inventory, vertical and competitor set. After a discovery call we provide a written proposal with scope, timeline and price.
Can the audit cover international markets?
Yes. Multi-region audits include localized page sets, language-specific intent clusters and regional competitor analysis.
