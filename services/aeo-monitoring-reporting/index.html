The discipline

AEO reporting is measurement with discipline

AEO performance reporting is not vanity dashboards. It is instrumented measurement of answer-engine visibility — snippet share, PAA capture, AI-answer citation frequency, voice citation, answer-engine referral traffic — against a baseline, with methodology disclosed and attribution discipline applied.

We install the measurement infrastructure (where your analytics permits), define the metrics and cadence, and produce monthly reports that show what changed, why, and what we recommend next. We distinguish changes caused by our work from seasonal, algorithmic and competitive effects.

The result is a defensible performance narrative you can take to leadership, finance and the board — not a sales deck dressed up as a report.

Common reporting problems

Reporting engagements begin when a business cannot explain its AEO performance, or suspects its current agency's reporting is misleading.

What we fix

  • No baseline — You cannot say whether AEO performance is improving or declining.
  • Vanity dashboards — Reports show metrics that look good but do not tie to business outcomes.
  • Methodology drift — Definitions change between reports — making comparisons meaningless.
  • No attribution discipline — All traffic gains are attributed to the agency; all losses to algorithm updates.
  • No commercial linkage — Reporting does not connect to pipeline, revenue or brand outcomes.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do you measure snippet share?
We track featured snippet appearance across a defined query set at consistent intervals, with methodology disclosed. We do not rely on third-party rank trackers alone.
Can you integrate with our existing GA4?
Yes. We use GA4 events (already configured on your site) and combine with answer-engine-specific instrumentation. We do not send PII to GA4.
How often do you report?
Monthly performance reports; quarterly business reviews. We can adjust cadence to your needs.
Do you attribute traffic to AEO work?
Yes — with discipline. We distinguish changes caused by our work from seasonal, algorithmic and competitive effects. Where attribution is uncertain, we say so.
Can you white-label reporting for our agency?
Yes. We produce white-labeled reports for partner agencies under NDA. Contact us to discuss.
