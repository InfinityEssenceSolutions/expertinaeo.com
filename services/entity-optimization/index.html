The discipline

Entity optimization is identity engineering

An entity is a uniquely identifiable thing — an organization, a person, a product, a place. Answer engines maintain knowledge graphs of entities and cite those they can identify with confidence. If your brand is weakly defined across the web, you will not be cited — regardless of content quality.

We strengthen entity signals in three layers: on-page schema (Organization, Person, Product with sameAs references), external entity building (Wikipedia, Wikidata, authoritative directory listings, social profiles with consistent NAP), and inter-page entity consistency (every page references the same canonical entity definitions).

The work is structural and slow-burning. Entity authority compounds over months and years — but once established, it is defensible and difficult for competitors to displace.

Common entity problems

Entity engagements begin when a business is absent from AI answers despite strong content, or appears with incorrect attribution.

What we fix

  • →No organization schema — Engines cannot identify your organization unambiguously across your pages.
  • →Inconsistent NAP — Name, address, phone vary across your site and external listings.
  • →No author profiles — Content is published without bylines, author schema or sameAs links.
  • →Weak external entity presence — Your brand has no Wikipedia, Wikidata or authoritative directory presence.
  • →Brand mis-attribution — AI assistants mention your brand but summarize it incorrectly.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will you create a Wikipedia page for my brand?
Only where your brand meets Wikipedia's notability guidelines. We do not create promotional pages and we do not guarantee acceptance. Where notability is established, we work with experienced editors.
How long does entity optimization take?
On-page schema and NAP consistency fixes show effects in weeks. External entity building (Wikipedia, knowledge graph presence) takes months and is not always achievable.
What is sameAs?
sameAs is a schema.org property that links your entity to its canonical identifiers elsewhere — Wikipedia, Wikidata, LinkedIn, Crunchbase, official social profiles. It tells engines 'this is the same organization as those.'
How do you measure entity authority?
We track knowledge graph presence, AI-answer citation frequency, branded-query AI-answer accuracy and external entity signal coverage.
Is entity optimization the same as SEO link building?
No. Link building improves page authority; entity optimization improves entity identity. Both matter, but they are different disciplines.
