The discipline

AEO consulting is strategy before tactics

Most AEO failures begin with tactics — adding schema, writing FAQ entries, chasing snippets — without a strategy that defines which topics, which intent clusters and which answer surfaces to compete on. Our consulting engagement reverses that order.

We diagnose your current answer visibility, map your competitive landscape, identify the topic clusters where you have the strongest right to win, and build a prioritized roadmap aligned to commercial impact. The output is a strategy document your team can execute against — with or without us in the room.

Common problems we solve

AEO consulting engagements typically start when a business has invested in content but cannot explain why it does or does not appear in AI answers, featured snippets or voice results.

What we fix

  • Inconsistent answer visibility — Some pages earn snippets, others do not, and nobody can explain the pattern.
  • No prioritized roadmap — Schema, content, technical fixes and entity work all compete for the same budget with no clear order.
  • Competitive blind spots — You do not know which competitors are winning which answer surfaces, or why.
  • Misaligned teams — Content, SEO, engineering and PR teams each touch AEO signals but no one owns the outcome.
  • No measurement framework — There is no baseline, no instrumentation and no agreed definition of success.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does AEO consulting cost?
Engagements are scoped to your inventory size, vertical and team structure. After a free 30-minute discovery call we provide a fixed-scope proposal with deliverables, timeline and price.
Do you execute the strategy, or only advise?
Both. Many clients engage us for strategy, then retain us for implementation. Others use the strategy to align in-house teams and external vendors. We support either model.
How is AEO consulting different from SEO consulting?
SEO consulting optimizes for ranked page results; AEO consulting optimizes for extracted and cited answers. The two disciplines share foundations but AEO adds deeper schema work, answer-first content patterns and entity-authority strategy.
Can you work with our existing SEO agency?
Yes. We frequently collaborate with in-house SEO teams and external agencies. Our role is to bring AEO-specific depth, not to displace existing relationships.
How long is a typical engagement?
Strategy engagements typically run 4–8 weeks. Ongoing advisory is monthly with a 6-month minimum.
