The discipline

Intent research is the foundation of any AEO program

Conventional keyword research identifies high-volume queries. Intent research goes further — classifying each query by user intent (informational, navigational, transactional, commercial investigation, comparative), identifying which intent stages reward answers, and mapping where your brand has the authority to win.

We combine search volume, SERP analysis (does the query reward a snippet? PAA? AI Overview?), competitive landscape (who currently owns the answer?) and your topical authority to produce a prioritized intent-cluster map. This map drives every downstream AEO decision — content, schema, entity, monitoring.

Without intent research, AEO tactics are guesses. With it, every page published has a defined purpose, target surface and expected outcome.

Common intent-research problems

Intent engagements begin when a business has invested in content but cannot explain which queries it should own or why.

What we fix

  • No intent map — You publish content without knowing which intent stages you target.
  • Wrong-intent content — Content written for informational queries targets commercial intent — or vice versa.
  • No answer-surface mapping — You do not know which queries reward snippets, PAA or AI Overviews.
  • Topic-cluster gaps — You have no plan for which clusters to invest in first.
  • No competitive intent map — You do not know which intent stages competitors own.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is intent research different from keyword research?
Keyword research identifies high-volume queries. Intent research classifies queries by user intent and answer-surface type, and maps where your brand has the right to win.
What data sources do you use?
Search engine results analysis, query extraction tools, your existing analytics, and competitive content audits. We do not rely on a single data source.
How often should intent research be refreshed?
Quarterly for high-velocity topics; semi-annually for stable ones. Intent shifts as user behavior and answer-surface features evolve.
Do you provide the queries in a usable format?
Yes. We deliver the intent-cluster map as a structured document (spreadsheet and PDF) your team can execute against.
Can intent research inform our content calendar?
Yes — that is one of its primary uses. The prioritized cluster map directly informs content, schema and entity investment.
