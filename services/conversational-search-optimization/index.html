The discipline

Conversational search is multi-turn answer discovery

Conversational search differs from classic query-result patterns. Users ask follow-up questions, refine intent mid-session and expect the assistant to maintain context. The content patterns that win a single SERP do not necessarily win a multi-turn chat — conversational assistants privilege extractable, declarative content with strong entity grounding.

We map the conversational paths users take through your topic, engineer content that supports multi-turn extraction (each section answers a self-contained sub-question), and strengthen the entity signals that make your brand unambiguously citable in chat responses.

The work spans content architecture, schema design, entity strategy and UX — particularly for chat surfaces where users can ask follow-up questions inline.

Common conversational-search problems

Engagements begin when a business is absent from chat-driven answers, or appears but is mis-attributed or summarized incorrectly.

What we fix

  • →AI chat absence — Major AI assistants do not cite your brand for queries in your topic.
  • →Mis-attribution — Chat assistants mention your brand but summarize your offering incorrectly.
  • →No multi-turn content — Your content answers one question per page, but chat users ask follow-ups.
  • →Weak entity grounding — Your brand is not unambiguously identifiable to chat assistants.
  • →No measurement — You do not know how often your brand appears in chat answers, or how.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which AI assistants do you optimize for?
Our work targets the major chat-driven surfaces — Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Claude and others. We prioritize based on your audience.
How is conversational search different from voice?
Voice is single-answer; conversational is multi-turn. Voice users ask one question; chat users ask follow-ups. The content patterns and schema strategies differ.
Can you guarantee AI Overview citations?
No. We engineer your content and signals to maximize citation probability and track frequency against a baseline. AI assistants make the final choice.
Do you correct existing mis-attribution?
Yes. We diagnose the cause (weak entity grounding, incorrect schema, source confusion) and implement corrections. Some corrections take weeks, others longer.
Is this the same as GEO?
There is heavy overlap. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and conversational search optimization share most techniques. We treat them as one workstream.
