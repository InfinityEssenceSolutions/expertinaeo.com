The discipline

AI visibility is measurement first

AI answer visibility is the discipline of measuring how often and how accurately your brand appears in AI-assistant responses. Without measurement, you cannot diagnose causes or improve outcomes. Most businesses have no idea whether they are cited, mis-cited or absent.

We instrument citation tracking across major AI assistants (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Claude), baseline your citation frequency and accuracy, and diagnose the underlying signals — entity strength, schema, content extractability, external authority — that drive AI citation.

The output is a defensible baseline, a measurement protocol and a prioritized improvement roadmap. We do not promise AI visibility — we measure it, explain it and improve it.

Common AI-visibility problems

Engagements begin when a business is uncertain whether AI assistants cite it accurately, or has anecdotal evidence of mis-attribution.

What we fix

  • No baseline — You cannot say with confidence how often AI assistants cite your brand.
  • Mis-attribution — AI assistants mention your brand but summarize it incorrectly.
  • Inconsistent citations — Some assistants cite you; others do not — no identifiable pattern.
  • Competitor dominance — Competitors are consistently cited where you are absent.
  • No diagnosis — You cannot explain why citations are weak or inaccurate.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do you measure AI citations?
We run a defined query set across major AI assistants at consistent intervals and capture citation frequency, position and accuracy. The methodology is disclosed and repeatable.
Can you guarantee AI citations?
No. We measure, diagnose and improve. AI assistants make the final citation choice. We track frequency over time against the baseline.
How often should I re-measure?
Quarterly for stable brands; monthly for high-velocity or competitive verticals. AI assistant behavior changes rapidly.
Do you correct mis-attribution?
Yes. We diagnose the cause (weak entity grounding, source confusion, incorrect schema) and implement corrections. Some corrections take weeks; others longer.
Which AI assistants do you track?
ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot and Claude. We prioritize based on your audience and geography.
