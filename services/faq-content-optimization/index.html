The discipline

FAQ optimization is structured answer engineering

Most FAQ sections are written by adding questions that customers ask and answering them in narrative form. The result reads well but is poorly suited to extraction. Engines cannot reliably identify the answer unit, so they pass over your page for a competitor's structured equivalent.

We re-engineer FAQ sections with explicit Q&A markup, declarative first-sentence answers, valid FAQPage schema and a refresh cadence that keeps coverage current. The result is FAQ content that engines reliably extract — for snippets, PAA, voice and AI summaries.

The work spans content, schema and instrumentation. We do not just write better FAQs; we engineer them for extraction and track their performance over time.

Common FAQ problems

FAQ engagements begin when a business has FAQ content that does not earn visibility, or has multiple disconnected FAQ sections with no schema.

What we fix

  • Narrative answers — Your FAQs answer questions but not in extractable declarative form.
  • No FAQPage schema — Your FAQ section has no JSON-LD markup — invisible to rich result eligibility.
  • Disconnected FAQ sections — FAQ content is scattered across product, support and blog pages with no consistency.
  • No intent coverage — Your FAQs answer what customers ask, not what they search for.
  • No refresh cycle — FAQ content goes stale; coverage drifts from current search behavior.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How many FAQ pages should I have?
Quality over quantity. A focused FAQ with 8–15 well-engineered questions on a single topic typically outperforms a sprawling 100-question FAQ. We help you scope.
Will FAQPage schema make my FAQs appear as rich results?
Eligibility does not guarantee appearance. FAQPage schema makes your FAQs eligible; content quality, authority and freshness determine whether engines display them.
Should FAQs be on one page or split across many?
Depends on intent. Single-topic FAQs work well on one page; multi-topic FAQs should be split. We recommend based on intent mapping.
How often should FAQ content be refreshed?
Quarterly for high-traffic FAQ pages; semi-annually for the rest. We set up refresh cadences as part of the engagement.
Do you write new FAQ content or just optimize existing?
Both. We can engineer existing content for extraction and produce specs for new FAQ content where coverage gaps exist.
