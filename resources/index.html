Reference

AEO concepts and definitions

What is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization is the practice of structuring content, technical signals and entity credibility so answer engines cite your brand as the source.

Learn more

AEO vs SEO vs GEO

Three related disciplines — each optimizes for a different discovery surface. AEO targets extracted and cited answers; SEO targets ranked pages; GEO targets AI summaries.

The five AEO pillars

Answer content, structured data, technical crawlability, entity signals and credibility. Our scoring framework covers all five.

E-E-A-T for AEO

Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness — the framework answer engines use to evaluate source quality. Decisive for YMYL.

Schema markup basics

JSON-LD structured data tells engines exactly what your page contains. Foundation for rich results and AI-answer citations.

Answer-first content

Engineering content so the answer appears in the first 40–80 words, in declarative form. Highest-leverage content change you can make.
