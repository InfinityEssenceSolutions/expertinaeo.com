Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy explains how ExpertinAEO, a product of Tapadia Tech, collects, uses and protects your data.

1. Who we are

ExpertinAEO is the Answer Engine Optimization practice of Tapadia Tech (established 2001). Our contact details are available on our contact page. We are the data controller for personal data collected through this website.

2. What data we collect

We collect the following personal data when you submit a form on our site:

  • Full name, business email, company name, country (required)
  • Phone/WhatsApp, industry, website URL, project description, budget, timeline, competitor URLs (optional or context-dependent)
  • Source URL, UTM parameters (marketing attribution)
  • IP address and user agent (collected automatically for security and rate-limiting)

When you use our free tools, we collect the URL you submit (for analysis) and aggregate, non-identifying usage data (tool name, score bucket, timestamp). We do not link tool usage to your personal identity unless you submit a form.

3. How we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the service you requested (free audit, consultation, etc.)
  • To send you information about our services where you have explicitly requested it
  • To improve our website, tools and content
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud and security incidents
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not share your personal data with third parties for their marketing purposes.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — you submitted a form with explicit consent to our processing
  • Contractual necessity — to provide the service you requested
  • Legitimate interest — to prevent abuse and improve our services
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable laws

5. Data retention

We retain enquiry form submissions for up to 24 months after our last interaction with you, after which we delete or anonymize the personal data. Tool usage logs (non-identifying) are retained for up to 12 months. Server logs (IP, user agent) are retained for up to 30 days for security.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal exceptions)
  • Restriction — request that we limit processing of your data
  • Portability — receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw consent at any time (does not affect prior processing)

To exercise any of these rights, email info@tapadiatech.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. International transfers

Your personal data may be processed in countries outside your jurisdiction (including the United States, United Kingdom, United Arab Emirates, India and Singapore). Where we transfer data outside the European Economic Area, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses, or another lawful transfer mechanism.

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including: HTTPS encryption in transit; access controls; rate limiting; honeypot fields; SSRF protection on tools; secure SMTP for email transmission; secrets stored outside public web directories.

10. Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us to request deletion.
